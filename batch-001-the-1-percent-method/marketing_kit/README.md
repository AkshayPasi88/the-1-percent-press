## Publishing checklist (per reel)

1. Upload the MP4 directly in the Instagram app (or via your Zernio queue).
2. Add a trending audio track in-app on low volume — this materially boosts reach.
3. Paste the matching caption from `captions_instagram.txt`; add 10–15 hashtags from `hashtags.txt`.
4. Cover frame: use the first frame of the punch section (giant gold "1%").
5. Link the ebook download in bio before the Monday slot goes live.
