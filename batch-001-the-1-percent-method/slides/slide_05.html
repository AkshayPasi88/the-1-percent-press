Systems, Not Sparks

Motivation arrives like a wave - loud, and then gone. People who change for good stop scheduling their lives around the wave.

Motivation is a mood

You don’t need to feel like it. You need a floor you can hit on your worst day.

Systems are the floor

A system is a decision made once: same time, same trigger, same tiny action. It runs whether you feel inspired or not.

Identity is the engine

Every action is a vote for a person. Write one sentence, and you are a writer. The habit is not the goal; the identity is.

You do not rise to the level of your motivation. You fall to the level of your systems.
