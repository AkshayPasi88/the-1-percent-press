The Two-Minute On-Ramp

Every habit has a two-minute version.
Master showing up before you master the workout.

‘Read before bed’
arrow_forward

becomes — read one paragraph.

‘Run three times a week’
arrow_forward

becomes — put on your running shoes.

‘Journal daily’
arrow_forward

becomes — write one sentence about today.

layers THE HABIT STACK
After I [existing habit],
I will [two-minute version].

Small isn’t the destination — it’s the on-ramp. The two-minute rule isn’t about doing less; it’s about becoming the person who shows up. Momentum is a personality trait you can install.
