Make It Obvious, Make It Easy

Environment is quiet autopilot. Willpower spends energy; design saves it.
In a well-designed room, discipline is mostly a furniture arrangement.

CUE IT UP
— make the good obvious

Put the guitar in the living room, not the case.

Lay tomorrow’s workout clothes on the chair tonight.

Keep the book on your pillow.

BREAK IT UP
— make the bad invisible

The phone charges in the kitchen overnight.

Snacks live on the top shelf — or not in the house at all.

Log out of the app that eats your evenings.
